Specialised services for complex jobs that need extra care.

From post-construction handovers to work at height, our specialised crews bring the right equipment, trained people and careful planning to every project.

Post-construction & post-renovation cleaning

Systematic debris removal, high-level dust mitigation on every surface including ceilings, walls and vents and specialised polishing to strip paint splatters and adhesive residue. Floors are scrubbed and restored so the space is genuinely safe and move-in ready.

High-rise window cleaning

Advanced rope access (abseiling) lets our trained technicians clean exterior windows and glass facades on high-rise and architecturally complex buildings. We are fully licensed by the Directorate of Occupational Safety and Health (DOSH), so every job is conducted in strict compliance with national safety protocols for work at heights along with facade restoration to remove pollutants, dust and residue.

Upholstery, mattress & carpet care

Deep-cleaning for couches and curtains that lifts allergens, stains and odours while preserving fabric integrity, plus intensive mattress sanitisation and dust-mite removal for a healthier sleeping environment, and specialised restoration for carpets and rugs of any size.

Professional fumigation

Safe, effective pest control tailored for homes, residential apartment common areas and commercial offices carried out with the same meticulous attention to detail as every other Brightside service.
