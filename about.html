About us

Brightside Cleaning Services ltd is a professional cleaning services company in Nairobi, Kenya, providing residential, office and commercial facility services.
Our trained teams, quality equipment and dependable scheduling keep homes, workplaces and shared spaces clean, safe and well maintained. Clients choose us because we arrive on time, deliver consistent results and take ownership of every job.

Our cleaning services include house cleaning, apartment cleaning and office cleaning, as well as common area cleaning for commercial buildings and residential apartment complexes. We also provide swimming pool cleaning and maintenance and gym cleaning, so recreational facilities stay hygienic and well kept for residents and members.

Beyond cleaning, we offer fumigation and pest control services in Nairobi for homes, offices and commercial properties, including treatment for cockroaches, bed bugs, termites and rodents. Our reliable garbage collection services in Nairobi keep your premises clean and compliant. With cleaning, pest control and waste management under one roof, you only need one facility services provider.
